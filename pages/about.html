About

Plutonium Network is a web platform providing access to games, applications, AI services, virtual machines, and more, all directly through your browser.

This platform is also meant for internet freedom. With the on-going, rising censorship in this world: all the way from GoGuardian on a school Chromebook to Europe's Digital Services Act, this site will help you evade that.
